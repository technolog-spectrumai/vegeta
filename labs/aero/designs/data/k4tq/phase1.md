# K4TQ — Stage 1: engineering design and feasibility

Generated by `labs/aero/designs/k4tq_sizing.py` (mocks ON (R12): numerical results labelled MOCK). Labels: ESTIMATE (hand formula / correlation), CALC (numerical model), MOCK (a mocked tool ran). No solver result is in this report.

## Verdict

- **Feasible on paper (ESTIMATE/MOCK)**: K4TQ-A takes off at 5.58 kg with 1 kg of payload, hovers on 871 W (T/W 1.84) and cruises at 21 m/s on 225 W; the R3 mission closes (margin 8 Wh).
- **Against the same-size quadcopters**: still-air range 53 km against 16 km (Q-13) and 18 km (Q-best) — ×2.9; top speed 32 against 21 m/s; but it hovers on 1.73× Q-best's power, carries 1.36 kg of wings and mechanisms, and adds four actuators, four hinges and a conversion to the failure modes.
- **Why**: the quad pays for lift with induced power at every speed; K4TQ pays it once in the hover and then flies on its wings at L/D ≈ 12. The price is the mass of the wings and roots, a worse hover (heavier, smaller discs than Q-best), and mechanical complexity. The tilt-wing wins as soon as the mission is mostly cruise; the quad wins for short hops and long hovers.

## Assumptions and uncertainties

- Masses of the made parts from areal/linear densities (wings 0.70 kg/m², fuselage 0.60 kg/m²); the roots itemised from catalogue parts — Stage 2 CAD replaces them.
- Motor model: first-order (Kv, R, I0 constant); the MN4014's I0 at 22 V and its mass with cable are to verify — the no-load loss dominates the cruise.
- Propellers: Boreas's generic section on a scaled Kestrel planform (constant pitch) — the real blades differ by ±10 % in thrust and power.
- Section constants from NeuralFoil at Re 2e5, n_crit 6; the built-up wing at 0.9 × cl_max.
- Tandem lattice: flat wake, no slipstream; the transition corridor: quasi-steady, momentum slipstream at 1.5 vᵢ on the blown span, post-stall flat plate — the free span's stall at intermediate tilt is what the CFD (R10) must check.
- Li-ion 6S2P Molicel P45B, usable 85 % × 95 % of nominal; loaded voltage 3.5 V/cell, sagged 3.3 V/cell.
- Roof wind: 8 m/s × 1.25 roof-edge speed-up × 1.45 gust factor; quasi-static overturning about the feet.
- Quadcopter references: same motors, pack, payload, cargo, avionics, parachute and margin; momentum theory with a μ² profile-power growth and a build-up CdA.
