# Race data

The racing quad flies Boreas's drive table from `designs/data/mini_raptor_drive_6S_5x4p3.json` (made by
`designs/exp/mini_raptor.py` `drive_table`) and Ogma's rotor law (`ogma.rotors`). The races lived in Piccolo from
2026-10-20 to 2026-10-21 and came home (MOVED.md); the frozen copy they carried there is gone.

`design_factors/`: `labs/aero/notebooks/races/race_design_factors.ipynb` writes what the laps say about the design
(`race_design_factors.json`, the comparison CSV) for the design notebooks (`designs/data/races/`).
